    Local receive windows

    Receive windows for local devices are sent in the join-accept: a change applies to devices that join after it. TTN sets its own for its devices. RX2 must be a frequency this gateway may transmit on (EU868 sub-bands).

    The power cap is 16.1 dBm EIRP (EU868, 14 dBm ERP) minus the antenna gain, never above the firmware ceiling. The gain table was measured for EU868 cards: with another card the real output power is unknown. A gain change restarts the radio (about 3 s).
