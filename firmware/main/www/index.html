      1. Register the gateway EUI above on your network server, with an EU868 channel plan.

    Receive windows for your devices

    Sent to each device when it joins: a change reaches devices that join after it. Your network server sets its own for its devices.

    Edit the channel plan

    A plan saved here is used until the network server sends its own: in network server and hybrid modes, the server's plan replaces it at the next connection. Local devices must join again after a change.
